## Why This Sponsor

**Strength 1: Best value per dollar.** 82.2 visibility points per $M, about 22% above the dataset average and the highest of any sponsor, at the lowest estimated cost.

**Strength 2: Lowest barrier to entry.** At an estimated $8.5M it is less than half the cost of the premium options, lowering the downside if the partnership underperforms.

**Strength 3: Consistent for its tier.** Low weekly volatility (std 8.8) means steady, predictable exposure rather than boom-or-bust.

## Risk Assessment

**Risk 1: Low absolute visibility** (likelihood: High)
- What could go wrong: At 699 total points (rank #4) it delivers far less raw exposure than the leaders, which may underwhelm a board expecting scale.
- Mitigation: Position as an efficient entry point, not a headline play.

**Risk 2: Smaller-team dependency** (likelihood: Medium)
- What could go wrong: Front Row Motorsports runs mid-pack; a weak season would further reduce exposure.
- Mitigation: Revisit if the team's competitiveness declines.

**Cost estimate uncertainty:** the $8.5M figure is a benchmark estimate. If actual cost is lower, efficiency improves; if higher, efficiency declines. Confirm with direct negotiation before finalizing.

## Connection to NY Racing

Love's fits a budget-conscious, ROI-first posture: it lets NY Racing enter with a defensible value story and prove the model before scaling up.
