**Recommendation Type:** Alternative (Premium)  **Analysis Date:** 2026-07-24

## Executive Summary

NAPA is the premium play: it ranks #1 in total visibility (1,219 points) but #4 in efficiency, reflecting the premium price of an elite Hendrick partnership. It maximizes exposure at the highest cost.

## Why This Sponsor

**Strength 1: Maximum total exposure.** Highest total visibility (1,219 points) and the most top-10 finishes (19), the best raw reach of any sponsor.

**Strength 2: Most consistent leader.** Lowest weekly volatility among the high-visibility sponsors (std 9.0), so the exposure is steady, not luck-driven.

**Strength 3: Elite brand association.** Partners the sport's most popular driver on an elite team, which carries prestige value beyond the raw score.

## Risk Assessment

**Risk 1: Lowest efficiency** (likelihood: High)
- What could go wrong: At 56.7 pts/$M (rank #4) NAPA pays a clear premium; a CFO will question the value per dollar.
- Mitigation: Only pursue if maximum brand awareness outweighs ROI.

**Risk 2: Highest cost commitment** (likelihood: High)
- What could go wrong: At an estimated $21.5M it is the largest investment and the biggest downside if the partnership underperforms.
- Mitigation: Ensure budget flexibility before committing.

**Cost estimate uncertainty:** the $21.5M figure is a benchmark estimate. If actual cost is lower, efficiency improves; if higher, efficiency declines. Confirm with direct negotiation before finalizing.

## Connection to NY Racing

NAPA fits an aggressive brand-awareness posture: it buys the most exposure and elite association, appropriate when growth and prestige outweigh ROI discipline.

NAPA Auto Parts represents the premium alternative, recommended only when maximum exposure is the goal and budget allows.
