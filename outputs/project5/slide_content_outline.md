## Slide 2: Executive Summary

**The Bottom Line**

- NAPA Auto Parts delivers the highest visibility (ranked #1 of 4 sponsors analyzed)
- Love's Travel Stops offers the best efficiency at 82 visibility points per million dollars
- FedEx is the only sponsor ranked top-2 on both visibility and efficiency

**Primary Recommendation**: FedEx, the balanced best bet.

---

## Slide 3: Project Approach

**How We Measured Sponsorship Visibility**

[Simple visual: Data Sources --> Scoring Model --> Rankings]

- Analyzed 4 primary sponsors across 36 races (2024 season)
- Composite score: race performance (40%), media (30%), social (20%), special events (10%)

**Key innovation**: first quantitative, apples-to-apples comparison of NASCAR sponsor visibility.

---

## Slide 4: Data and Limitations

**What We Measured**

| Category | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Race Performance | Kaggle, Racing Reference | 36 races |
| Social Media | Google Trends, YouTube API | Fan engagement |
| News Coverage | Manual search | Quality-weighted |
| Costs | Industry estimates | 15-20% uncertainty |

**Important caveat**: cost estimates are approximations, not actual contract values.

## Slide 5: Finding #1 - Highest visibility is not best value

**The most-seen sponsor is the least efficient.**

[Visualization: efficiency_rankings.png]

*So what?*: NAPA leads on exposure but ranks last on value per dollar; Love's is the reverse.
Paying more does not always buy more visibility.

---

## Slide 6: Finding #2 - FedEx is the balanced sweet spot

**FedEx is the only sponsor strong on both dimensions.**

[Visualization: visibility_rankings.png]

*So what?*: FedEx ranks #2 in visibility and #2 in efficiency, capturing 99.7% of the top
sponsor's exposure at a materially better value. It minimizes the trade-off.

---

## Slide 7: Finding #3 - The recommendation is robust

**The ranking holds no matter how we weight the model.**

[Visualization: sensitivity_analysis.png]

*So what?*: Across four different weighting scenarios the ranking moves at most one position.
The conclusion does not depend on our exact assumptions.

## Slide 8: Recommendations

**Who Should NY Racing Pursue?**

**Primary Recommendation: FedEx**
- Visibility: 1,215 points (Rank #2)
- Efficiency: 65.7 pts/$M (Rank #2)
- Why: near-max exposure at better value; the most wins (3) of any sponsor

**Alternative: Love's Travel Stops**
- Best for teams prioritizing efficiency (best value per dollar, lowest cost)
